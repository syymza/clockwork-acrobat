Controls & mechanism

Tap a named lever once to engage or hold that motion, then drag the crank to drive it. The lever’s position and colored handle show its state. Tap the brass latch to open Inspection; tap the door to close it. On a mouse or keyboard, hover or focus a control for its next action. The buttons below offer the same controls on touch and with a keyboard. Music is silent until you engage its lever and turn the crank; disengaging it silences all notes.

A shared seven-gear drive powers Bow (1:2), Balance (1:3), Tumble (1:4), Music (1:2), and the new Jump cam (1:1). Start with Jump only: one crank revolution produces one jump. The blue cam and lift rod rise together. Each other lever adds a separate motion through an ideal friction clutch. Disengaged cams hold their position. Combine lanes to alter the performance; reverse the crank to retrace it.

Focus an on-box control and press Enter or Space. The crank accepts arrows (15°), Shift + arrows (7.5°), Page Up/Down (90°). Drag bare wood to orbit; pinch / wheel to zoom. No autoplay. Opening the door frames the mechanism; turning a motion lane returns to the full-motion overview. Music-only cranking keeps the closer inspection view. The hinged door opens outward below the acrobat.

Hover or focus a lever to trace its real drive route. A disengaged clutch leaves a physical gap; only its input path is tinted. Pins, comb pitches and notes share one score. At very fast turns the melody is evenly sampled into a maximum 0.42-second queue to avoid bursts; tine movement follows the scheduled notes. This is a kinematic teaching model: simplified spur teeth, ideal clutches and programmed cam tracks, with no inertia, force or tooth-contact simulation. A telescoping clearance guide keeps combined poses above the cabinet.
